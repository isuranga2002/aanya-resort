Welcome to Aanya

A hidden sanctuary where the rainforest meets still blue water

Off Hewainna Road, past the rubber estates and village paddy fields beyond Avissawella, the trees open up onto a cluster of glass-fronted A-frame villas. Each one looks out over its own pool, with the canopy rolling away to the hills on every side.

Aanya is small, quiet and personal. Spend the day floating above the treetops, eat home-style Sri Lankan food cooked fresh in our kitchen, and watch the lights come on around the pool after dark. Our team will look after you as if you were family.

Around Avissawella

Beyond the villa gates

Avissawella was once the seat of the 16th-century Sitawaka kingdom. Today it's surrounded by rivers, rubber estates and forest, and there's plenty to see nearby.

01

Seethawaka Botanical Garden

A wet-zone botanic garden with forest trails, rare native plants and quiet streams.

02

Berendi Kovil

Stone ruins of a temple begun by King Rajasinghe I of Sitawaka. A short trip into the island's history.

03

Kelani River

The river that flows past Avissawella, with riverside walks and bathing spots.

04

Kitulgala

White-water rafting, canyoning and jungle treks, roughly an hour's drive up the valley.

05

Leisure World

A water and theme park on the Colombo–Avissawella road, good for a family day.

06

Waterfalls & Trails

The hills around Avissawella hide several waterfalls and village trails. Ask our team for directions.

Good to Know

Frequently asked

How do I make a booking?

The quickest way is WhatsApp on 071 457 6474. Use the form on this page and it opens WhatsApp with your dates already typed in. You can also call 077 647 4764.

Does every villa have a private pool?

Yes. When you book a villa, its pool is for your group only.

What are the day-out hours?

Day outings run from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM and include lunch and dessert. If you'd like to stay into the evening, ask about an overnight package.

Can you arrange a birthday or proposal surprise?

Yes. Our team regularly sets up flowers, cakes and decorations. Let us know what you have in mind when you book.

Can we come by bus?

The access road after Puwakpitiya is narrow, so we recommend cars or vans. Large buses can't reach the resort.

Is there Wi-Fi?

Yes, free Wi-Fi is available. Mobile signal in the hills can be patchy, so Wi-Fi is the most reliable way to stay connected.
